# 📊 HR Executive Overview & Workforce Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project presents an end-to-end HR Analytics solution built to evaluate workforce composition, compensation patterns, and turnover dynamics. By synthesizing employee metrics, the dashboard enables HR leadership to make data-backed decisions regarding retention strategies, compensation allocation, and workforce planning.

---

## 📈 Key Metrics (KPIs)
* **Total Workforce:** 1,000 Employees (524 Active | 476 Exited)
* **Attrition & Retention Rates:** 48% Attrition Rate vs. 52% Retention Rate
* **Average Tenure:** 5.24 Years across the organization
* **Financial Overhead:** $27.30M Basic Salary | $10.99M Net Salary | $1.73M Total Bonuses

---

## 🔍 Key Findings & Business Insights

### 1. Attrition Drivers & Tenure Risk
* **Primary Exit Reasons:** Retirement (106) and Termination (95) are the leading reasons for employee departure, followed by Contract Expirations (94) and Resignations (90).
* **Tenure Vulnerability:** Exits peak significantly among staff with **7–10 years** (37 exits) and **3–4 years** (35 exits) of service.

### 2. Workforce Demographics & Employment Structure
* **Gender Distribution:** Exited personnel comprise 167 Females (35.08%), 156 Males (32.77%), and 153 Non-Binary employees (32.14%).
* **Employment Types:** Full-time and Contract workers represent the core of the workforce compared to Intern and Part-time tiers.

### 3. Compensation Allocation
* **Generational Earnings:** Millennials (Ages 30–39) represent the highest compensation pool at **$2.9M**, followed by Baby Boomers (Ages 50–59) at **$2.8M** and Gen X (Ages 40–49) at **$2.6M**.
* **Job Title Distribution:** Manager and Assistant-level roles account for the highest salary and total compensation allocations.

---

## 🛠️ Data Pipeline & Methodology
1. **Data Cleaning & Transformation:** Cleaned raw HR records, categorized tenure into actionable age/tenure brackets, and standardized exit reasons.
2. **Data Modeling & DAX:** Formulated measures for dynamic KPI tracking, including rolling attrition, retention benchmarks, net salary calculations, and bonus-to-deduction ratios.
3. **Dashboard UX Design:** Structured a multi-tab executive overview featuring balanced visual hierarchies, department slicers, and cross-filtering capabilities.

---

## 💡 Strategic HR Recommendations
* **Mid-Tenure Retention Interventions:** Implement targeted career development plans for employees reaching the 3–4 year mark to curb voluntary turnover.
* **Succession Planning:** Establish proactive hiring and knowledge transfer pipelines for departments facing heavy retirement exits (7–10+ year tenure).
